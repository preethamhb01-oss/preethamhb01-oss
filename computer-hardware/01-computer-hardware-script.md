## What is Hardware?
"Computer hardware means the physical parts of a computer that we can see and touch. Your CPU, RAM, motherboard, SSD, keyboard and monitor are all examples of hardware."

## Hardware vs Software
"Hardware is the physical equipment. Software is the collection of programs and instructions that run on that equipment. For example, your keyboard is hardware, while Windows and Chrome are software."

## Main Parts
"Let's quickly understand the major internal components.

The CPU, or Central Processing Unit, executes instructions and performs calculations.

RAM is the computer's temporary working memory. When you run applications, they use RAM while they are active.

The motherboard is the main circuit board that connects the major components.

The GPU, or Graphics Processing Unit, processes graphics and can also handle other highly parallel workloads.

An SSD or HDD is used to store your operating system, applications and personal files.

And the PSU, or Power Supply Unit, provides the regulated electrical power required by the computer."

## Cooling
"All these components can produce heat while working, so computers use heat sinks, fans, thermal paste and sometimes liquid cooling to control temperatures."

## How Everything Works Together
"So how do these parts work together? When you open an application, its data is loaded from storage into RAM. The CPU processes instructions, the GPU may process graphics, and the final result is sent to your display."

## Closing
"So remember: CPU for processing, RAM for temporary working memory, storage for saving data, GPU for graphics and parallel workloads, motherboard for connecting components, and PSU for power.

This is the foundation of computer hardware. In the next videos, we can go deeper into each component."
