# PreeTech — Episode 01
## Let's Understand Computers From Zero!

**Format:** Beginner-friendly YouTube video  
**Language:** English  
**Audience:** People with little or no computer background  
**Episode goal:** Introduce a computer setup, explain its basic parts, and share the vision for the PreeTech series.

---

## 1. Opening: What is a computer?

Have you ever used a computer to watch videos, study, play games, browse the internet, or do office work?

A computer is an electronic device that takes information (input), works with that information (processing), and gives us a result (output). It can also save information for later use.

You don't need a computer science degree to understand how it works. In this series, we'll learn about computers and everyday technology step by step, using simple language and examples.

**Visual:** Start with a wide shot of a complete desktop setup.

## 2. A basic desktop computer setup

A typical desktop setup may include:
- **Monitor** — the screen where we see text, pictures, videos, and applications.
- **Keyboard** — lets us type letters, numbers, symbols, and commands.
- **Mouse** — lets us move the pointer, click, select, and scroll.
- **Computer cabinet/case** — the enclosure that holds many important internal components.

These parts have different jobs, but they work together to help us use the computer.

![Computer setup with monitor, keyboard and mouse](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/RGB_desktop_computer_setup_with_keyboard_and_monitor.jpg)

## 3. Monitor — where we see the result

The monitor is the display screen. When we open a website, watch a video, write a document, or play a game, the monitor shows us the visual result.

**Simple example:** A monitor is similar to a television screen, but it displays the output of a computer.

**Visual idea:** Show the monitor while opening a webpage or a document.

## 4. Keyboard — how we enter information

The keyboard helps us enter letters, numbers, symbols, and commands. We use it to type our name, write messages, prepare documents, and create computer programs.

**Simple example:** Type a short sentence on screen and point out the Enter and Backspace keys.

## 5. Mouse — how we point and select

The mouse is a pointing device. We use it to move the pointer on the screen, click icons, open applications, select items, and scroll through pages.

**Simple example:** Move the pointer and click an application icon.

![Computer with mouse and keyboard](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Computer_with_mouse_and_keyboard.png)

*Image credit: Hacker19374, CC BY-SA 4.0. Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Computer_with_mouse_and_keyboard.png).*

## 6. Computer cabinet/case — what is inside?

The computer cabinet, also called the computer case or tower, is the enclosure that contains many of the computer's internal parts. People sometimes call the whole cabinet “the CPU,” but technically the **CPU is the processor chip inside the computer**, not the cabinet itself.

Depending on the computer, the cabinet can contain:
- **CPU/processor:** executes instructions and performs calculations.
- **RAM:** temporarily holds information that programs are using.
- **Storage drive:** keeps files and applications when the computer is switched off.
- **Motherboard:** connects the internal components.
- **Power supply and cooling:** provide power and help manage heat.
- **Graphics card (in some computers):** helps process graphics and other suitable workloads.

We will explain these components slowly in future episodes. For this first video, viewers only need to understand that the cabinet houses the parts that make the computer work.

**Visual idea:** Show the outside of a computer cabinet first, then a clear labelled diagram of its internal components. Do not open a powered computer for filming.

## 7. How the parts work together

Imagine searching for something on the internet:
1. Use the mouse to open a browser.
2. Use the keyboard to type what you want to search.
3. The computer processes the instructions and loads the page.
4. The monitor displays the result.

That simple task shows how the external parts and the internal computer work together.

## 8. The aim of the PreeTech series

PreeTech is starting with one simple goal: **make technology easier to understand for everyone.**

You don't need a technical background to join this journey. We'll begin with the basics and gradually explore computer components, laptops, operating systems, useful applications, computer specifications, and everyday technology.

Each episode will focus on one topic, explain it in simple English, and use examples or pictures to make it easier to understand.

If you are curious about technology, this series is for you. Let's learn together, one step at a time.
